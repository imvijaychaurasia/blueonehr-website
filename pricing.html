Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?
Your free trial gives you full access to core HR modules for 15 days with up to 25 employees no credit card required. At the end of the trial, you can continue with a paid plan or export your data.
Can I migrate from our existing HRMS?
Yes. Our onboarding team helps you import employee data, historical attendance, and payroll records from CSV exports or common HRMS formats. Migration support is included in Growth and Enterprise plans.
Is our data stored in India?
Yes. BlueOneHR runs on India-region infrastructure. All employee data, payroll records, and documents are stored and processed within India, keeping you compliant with Indian data localisation requirements.
What support do we get?
Free Trial includes email support. Growth plans get priority email and a dedicated onboarding call. Enterprise customers get a named account manager, phone support, and an SLA with guaranteed response times.
Can I pay monthly instead of annually?
We offer both monthly and annual billing. Annual plans come with a significant discount. Contact us to discuss the option that works best for your budget.
