Why we built BlueOneHR

Most HRMS software in India is either painfully expensive (SAP, Oracle) or painfully outdated (desktop applications from 2005 that require a local IT team to run).

Fast-growing Indian companies from 50-person startups to 1,500-person mid-market businesses are stuck patching together spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and legacy payroll software that breaks every June.

BlueOneHR is our answer: a modern, cloud-native HR platform built specifically for Indian compliance (PF, ESI, TDS, PT), Indian payroll structures, and Indian working patterns at a price point that makes sense.
