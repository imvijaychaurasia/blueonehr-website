Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?
The free trial gives you full access to all modules — including payroll, recruitment, performance management, workspace apps, and employee KYC — for 15 days with up to 25 employees. No credit card required. At the end of the trial you can upgrade to a paid plan or export your data.
What is the difference between Business Starter and Business Standard?
Business Starter covers all core HR operations: employee management, attendance & leave, payroll with statutory compliance, assets, offboarding, HR letters, help desk, and reports.

Business Standard adds the full talent suite: recruitment pipeline & ATS, structured onboarding workflows, performance reviews & OKRs, and project management — making it ideal for growing teams that are actively hiring.
Can I migrate from our existing HRMS?
Yes. Our onboarding team helps you import employee data, historical attendance, and payroll records from CSV exports or common HRMS formats. Migration support is included in Business Standard and Enterprise plans.
Is our data stored in India?
Yes. BlueOneHR runs on India-region infrastructure. All employee data, payroll records, and documents are stored and processed within India, keeping you compliant with Indian data localisation requirements.
What support is included?
Business Starter includes email support. Business Standard adds priority support and a dedicated onboarding call. Enterprise customers get a named account manager, phone support, and an SLA with guaranteed response times.
Can I pay monthly instead of annually?
We offer both monthly and annual billing. Annual plans come with a significant discount. Contact us to discuss the option that works best for your budget.
